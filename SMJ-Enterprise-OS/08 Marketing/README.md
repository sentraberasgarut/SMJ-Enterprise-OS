## Core Strategic Principle: Consultation Marketing

The WhatsApp consultation is the product being marketed; rice is the
solution delivered after. Every campaign must serve at least one of:
Educate, Build Trust, Generate WhatsApp Conversations, Increase Authority,
Create Referrals, Increase Repeat Orders. **Primary KPI for every campaign:
Consultation Requests** — not reach, not engagement.

## Recommended First Live Campaign (never executed)

"What's Your Rice Actually Doing?" (Household Diagnostic) — chosen over
Myth-Busting because it converts to WhatsApp consultations most directly
and has zero dependency on unfinished assets (real testimonials still don't
exist).

## Platform Roles

Threads (familiarity, 7x/week) → Instagram (authority, 5x/week, 40% edu /
30% story / 20% authority / 10% promo) → Facebook (community, 3-5x/week,
never copy-pasted from Instagram) → WhatsApp (the consultation itself,
never a sales push).
