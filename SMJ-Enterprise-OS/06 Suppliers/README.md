# 06 Suppliers

## SBGA Supplier Philosophy

SBGA doesn't buy "good rice" — it buys "the right rice," evaluated
per-batch against the Four-Sense Protocol (sight, smell, touch, taste —
tested both immediately and 2-4 hours after cooking, since real service
windows expose rice over time). Decades-long regional (Garut) sourcing
relationships; per-batch evaluation, not blanket trust of any supplier.

## Toko Sembako Sejahtera Suppliers (real data)

6 suppliers, 37 restock batches recorded, 44 restock line items. Real
per-supplier spend and payment-status analysis: `11 Data Platform/scripts/analytics/05_supplier.sql`.
All observed restock batches in this data show fully paid status (no
outstanding supplier balances in the current export window).
