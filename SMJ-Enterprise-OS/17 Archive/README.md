## Archival Principle

Nothing referenced above was deleted from its original location. This
folder exists as an index of "things that used to be the authoritative
version of something, and where to still find them" — not as a copy of
those things. This avoids the exact duplication risk the refactor was
meant to eliminate, while still satisfying "no information may be lost."
