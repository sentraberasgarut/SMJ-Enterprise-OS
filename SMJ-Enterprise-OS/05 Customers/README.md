# 05 Customers

## SBGA Customer Segments (12-month priority, CEO-confirmed)

**Primary:** Household, Reseller. **Secondary/occasional only:** Restaurant.
(Hotel and Catering are documented in the original persona set but not
prioritized in the current 12-month window.)

## The Reseller Lesson (Foundational Institutional Wisdom)

A reseller once chose rice based purely on margin. His customers rejected it
(poor visual quality, weak fragrance) and the inventory came back. Standing
principle since: think "two customers ahead" — consider not just the
immediate buyer, but their customer too. This single case appears across
Customer Persona, Product Catalog, Brand Voice, and the Objection Library —
it is the most load-bearing piece of institutional knowledge in the entire
enterprise.

## Toko Sembako Sejahtera Customers (real data)

8 registered customers, 59% of all 316 invoices have no customer attached
(anonymous walk-in retail sales — normal for a grocery store, not a data
defect). Real per-customer purchase behavior (total spent, frequency,
first/last purchase) is queryable: `11 Data Platform/scripts/analytics/04_customer.sql`.

**This is a concrete gap vs. SBGA's ambition:** SBGA has an extensive,
built CRM (`09 Sales/`) with zero live customer records; Toko Sembako
Sejahtera has real customer transaction history but no CRM at all.
