# Sentra Telur Keluarga — Business Unit Profile

**Role:** Future expansion of CV Sederhana Maju Jaya
**Status:** Not launched. Name only.

## What Is Known

⚠️ **Knowledge Gap:** No operational data, no built systems, no confirmed
business model exist for this unit in any prior document. The name
("Telur" = eggs in Indonesian) suggests an egg/poultry-related business,
consistent with "Telur" (eggs) already being the top-selling product by
volume at Toko Sembako Sejahtera (see `02 Business Units/Toko Sembako
Sejahtera/README.md`) — this may indicate a deliberate vertical-integration
strategy (supplying/producing the eggs the retail store already sells
heavily), but **this is an inference, not a confirmed fact**, and is
flagged as such rather than stated as strategy.

## Recommendation

Do not build any systems for this unit until the CEO provides a founding
brief similar to what exists for SBGA (origin story, philosophy, target
customer, initial scope). Building AI infrastructure ahead of a defined
business model would repeat the risk already flagged for SBGA (over-built
systems relative to live operations) at an even earlier stage.
