# Sentra Beras Garut Asli (SBGA) — Business Unit Profile

**Role:** Growth initiative of CV Sederhana Maju Jaya (previously, in prior
documentation, incorrectly treated as "the company" itself — corrected in
this refactor)
**Business type:** Rice distribution, positioned as an AI-first, knowledge-driven advisor model

## Origin Story

SBGA began with 200 kilograms of rice. The founder had spent years helping
run a family restaurant business (five branches, since 1987). The pivotal
moment: his mother offered him capital to build his own rice business
instead of continuing to pay him for restaurant work. That decision — an
act of holding-level capital allocation, in retrospect — is SBGA's true
origin point.

## Core Philosophy

"There is no universally best rice. There is only the best rice for a
specific kitchen, menu, workflow, and customer base." SBGA competes on
diagnostic expertise, not price — the Trusted Advisor Model, never
recommending based on margin alone (see "The Reseller Lesson," a real
cautionary case documented in `05 Customers/`).

**The Line SBGA Will Never Cross:** never recommend based on margin alone at
the customer's expense; never claim a single variety is universally best;
never fabricate urgency; never let AI publish without human approval.
