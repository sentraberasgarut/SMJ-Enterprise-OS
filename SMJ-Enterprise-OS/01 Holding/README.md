## Role of the Holding Company

The holding company's function, as distinct from any individual business
unit, is:
1. Capital allocation across business units (funding the SBGA growth
   initiative appears to have been a holding-level decision, per the
   original SBGA founding story — see `02 Business Units/SBGA/`)
2. Oversight — the CEO operates at the holding level, directing both the
   current cash engine and the growth initiative
3. Shared infrastructure ownership — the AI Workforce (`12 AI Workforce/`)
   and Technology Stack (`13 Technology/`) are holding-level assets used
   across business units, not owned by any single unit
