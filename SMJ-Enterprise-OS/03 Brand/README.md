## SBGA Brand Voice

**In one sentence:** SBGA writes like a knowledgeable, honest advisor talking
to someone they respect — never like a salesperson trying to close a deal.

**Do:** Ask before recommending. Name trade-offs honestly. Use specific
sensory language over generic superlatives ("holds its texture after 3
hours" not "the best rice!"). Ground claims in real behavior. Speak to a
specific reader (not "everyone"). Educate first. Let expertise carry the
authority.

**Don't:** Never lead with price. Never claim one variety is universally
"the best." Never manufacture urgency or scarcity. Never hide a weakness to
make a sale. Never use generic commodity-marketing language without a
specific, checkable claim behind it.

## Marketing Philosophy (The Four Pillars)

1. **Education Over Promotion** — content teaches something true, even when also promoting
2. **Trust Over Transaction** — never optimize a single conversion at the expense of long-term credibility
3. **Diagnosis Before Recommendation** — content helps a prospect self-diagnose before introducing a product
4. **Long-Term Customer Value Over Price Competition** — never lead with discounts or price comparisons
